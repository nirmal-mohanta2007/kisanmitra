# Kisan Mitra - Demo Script (3-Minute Pitch)

## Introduction (30 seconds)
"Hello everyone, meet Ramesh Nayak, a farmer who often spends days waiting at the Mandi to sell his crops, losing money and time. Today, we are presenting *Kisan Mitra*, a platform to fix this broken supply chain."

## The Farmer App (1 minute)
"Here is the Kisan Mitra app. Ramesh books a slot from his phone. Notice the smart ETA engine—it tells him exactly when to arrive. No more waiting overnight. Once he arrives, a token is generated and he is placed in the digital queue."

## The Operator View (1 minute)
"Now, let's switch to the Operator Dashboard. The operator sees Ramesh's token and calls him forward. Quality checks and weighing are recorded digitally. The state machine ensures no steps are skipped. Watch as we finalize the weight and trigger the payment."

## Conclusion (30 seconds)
"The transaction is complete. Payment is processed transparently based on MSP. With Kisan Mitra, we bring efficiency, transparency, and dignity back to our farmers. Thank you."
